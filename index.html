Process

Research-led thinking, expressive outcomes.

  1. 01

    Listen closely

    Audience, context and business intent first, using the 5Ws and 1H to ask the right questions.

  2. 02

    Define the problem

    A clear problem keeps every design decision focused and purposeful.

  3. 03

    Shape the system

    Ideas first, pixels later. Type, colour and hierarchy built into scalable systems.

  4. 04

    Refine & iterate

    Polish, test and tweak with client feedback until the work feels clear and effortless.
